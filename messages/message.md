# Welcome to the Patcher v3!

Thanks for sticking around! Version 3 is the biggest update the Patcher has had, rebuilt from the ground up to be smarter, sturdier and ready for whatever the game throws at it next.

## What's new

- **Patching that keeps up with the game.** The Patcher now finds what it needs inside the game by itself. Game updates should no longer break it, or leave you waiting for a fix.
- **GOG support.** Bought Stellaris on GOG? The Patcher can now find and launch it through GOG Galaxy on Windows and macOS.
- **Play it your way.** The first time you patch, you'll be asked where your game comes from: **Steam**, **GOG** or **Generic**. Generic covers everything else, including apps like Heroic, Lutris or Bottles, which you can set up to launch the game from the Patcher.
- **One simple patch.** The old separate patches are now a single one that keeps achievements on, tidies away the yellow version text and keeps the crash reporter quiet.
- **Patch from anywhere.** The Patcher works out whether your game is the Windows or Linux version, so you can even patch it from a different system. There's now a native macOS app too.
- **Easier cleanup.** Clear out old game backups with **Delete Backups**, and old save backups with **Manage Save Backups**.

## Better all round

- A progress bar shows what the Patcher is doing while it works.
- The options window is tidier, with **General Utilities** for the Patcher itself and **Game Utilities** for your game. Press **Cancel** to undo anything you changed.
- **Fix Save** is more reliable, and a new **Quick Tips & FAQs** window (the **?** button) answers the most common questions.
- Plenty of fixes under the hood, so things should feel smoother and crash less.

## Good to know

- After a game update, just patch again.
- Launching from the Patcher is optional. You can always start the game from Steam, GOG Galaxy or your usual launcher.
- You can see this message again any time from **Show Information Message**, in the options under **General Utilities**.

If something doesn't work as expected, please let me know on GitHub. Enjoy your achievements, and have fun conquering the galaxy!
